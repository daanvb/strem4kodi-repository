# App audit — 14 October 2026

This audit covers the pending Up Next work and subsequent startup, browsing,
search, playback-service and episode-progress changes on top of published 1.8.97.
These changes are included in release 1.8.98. No native Fire TV or other Kodi device was attached.

## Findings fixed

| Area | Verified issue | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal stop | A forced final sample could replace the last valid position with Kodi's cleared zero clock or a newly started video's clock. | Preserve the previous owned sample when the final clock is reset or the playing file has changed. Test normal stop of S11E12 through saved state to a 50% card. |
| Episode selection | The synopsis followed the selected card, while the primary Resume button/target kept the saved series episode. | Derive Play/Resume and offset from the selected episode. Resolve selection again on the primary click so rapid navigation cannot use the preceding target. |
| Rewatch progress | A watched-history bitmap could suppress a new partial rewatch. | Show the current, explicitly unfinished session's exact-episode progress; preserve completed and sentinel behavior. |
| Background service | An exception in one tick could terminate all observers, sync and progress processing. | Isolate individual ticks and queue/upload work; retry on subsequent ticks and rate-limit technical-only failure logging. |
| Service shutdown | Cleanup referenced the removed format_discovery worker, and one cleanup error could block subsequent cleanup. | Remove the invalid reference and isolate cleanup for live recovery, display, Up Next, skip controls and the service marker. |
| Native navigation | Home-to-details waited for closure but still opened details after a timeout. | Stop allocation on an unretired Home window and record the bounded timeout checkpoint. |
| Search typing | Each cache lookup copied rows for all cached queries, including unrelated queries. | Copy only the relevant query/prefix rows; preserve catalogue retention and ranking. |
| Long browsing | Full hero metadata accumulated without a bound. | Keep 64 recent hero entries with thread-safe reads/writes/clear. Persistent metadata and saved lists are unaffected. |

The pending Up Next improvements are included: titles remain visible with thumbnail
spoiler protection; repaired titles carry into the loading/player payload; episode
metadata bypasses optional series quality enrichment; slow credits requests do not
hold the title. One source search prepares during the closing minutes and a recent,
session/provider-scoped preflight can be reused. Preparation never starts playback.
Natural-end autoplay, cancellation, identity checks and source recovery remain.

checks and final complete suite pass.

## Progress rendering evidence and remaining device checks

Kodi Omega calculates ListItem.PercentPlayed from the native resume bookmark:
[Kodi VideoGUIInfo.cpp](https://github.com/xbmc/xbmc/blob/Omega/xbmc/guilib/guiinfo/VideoGUIInfo.cpp#L48).
The app supplies both offset and total duration and its visibility property from
the same exact-episode calculation. The progress track and fill textures have
matching 170×8 dimensions. No speculative graphics workaround was applied.

The user's Fire TV photos show a Resume label on multiple selected episodes; the
user reports normal stop on Bob's Burgers S11E12, first losing progress and later
saving after a seek without a visible bar. The audit fixes the proven target mismatch,
zero-clock overwrite path and rewatch suppression. It does not establish which of
those occurred on the physical TV; the saved report/state was not inspected here.

Retest S11E12: stop around halfway, return to details, verify its bar/percentage and
Resume, move to an unstarted episode and verify Play, then return to S11E12 and resume
at the saved position. Also retest Up Next title and handoff speed, Back to Search,
Cancel, trailers and sports reconnect. Decoder crashes, actual frame rate, firmware
differences and abrupt process/power termination cannot be proven by these tests.
The existing periodic progress checkpoint still limits what can survive a forced
termination without a normal stop callback. No speed percentage is claimed.
